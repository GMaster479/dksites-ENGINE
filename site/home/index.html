Why not just ask ChatGPT to build it?

A chatbot can write the code for a web page. That’s the easy part. Then you still have to:

  • buy a web address and set up its settings
  • find hosting, put the files there, and turn on the padlock
  • make sure it has your real hours, prices and reviews, not made-up ones
  • make it work on every phone and pass Google’s checks
  • change it later without breaking what was already right

DK Sites Builder does all of that, and it doesn’t make things up.

It runs on a design rulebook written by a web designer with over a decade of experience who still builds sites for local businesses every week. The rules cover layout, type, color contrast, menus, photos, phones, accessibility and how Google reads your site. One rule above all: never invent a price, a menu item or an hour you didn’t give us.

Questions people ask

Is there a monthly fee?

No. You pay once for the year when your site goes live. Building and previewing it is free.

Do I own my website?

Yes. Your business content and the site built for it are yours, and you can ask for a copy of your site’s files at any time. A web address we register for you is yours too, and we’ll transfer it to you on request.

What if I don’t like what it makes?

Ask for changes, as many as you like, or start over with a fresh design. Each change only touches what you asked for. Nothing is charged until you choose to go live.

Does it include email?

Not inboxes. If you already have email at your web address (like you@yourbusiness.com through Gmail or Outlook), we copy its settings when you connect the domain so it keeps working.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Auto-renew is off unless you turn it on, and you can switch it off any time from your dashboard. If you don’t renew, your site goes offline at the end of the year you paid for.

Will it show up on Google?

Every site is built so Google can read it properly: real page titles and descriptions, your business details in the format Google uses, and links to your social profiles. No one can promise a ranking, but the basics are done right.
